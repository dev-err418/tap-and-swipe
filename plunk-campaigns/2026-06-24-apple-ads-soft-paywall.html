Somewhere along the way I convinced myself trials were always the move.

Not sure why. Because right now I am seeing the opposite.

Right now the setup working best on one of my apps is stupidly simple:

  • exact-match Apple Search Ads keywords
  • soft paywall after onboarding
  • no free trial
  • direct yearly subscription

The paywall is still soft. People can close it and use the app. But if the intent is high enough, a surprising number of people are just buying directly.

The lesson for me is pretty simple: don't blindly copy the usual mobile app funnel.

Trials are great when people need time to understand the value. But for search traffic, the user already typed the problem into the App Store. Sometimes the intent is already there.

In that case, adding a free trial can also add one more delay between intent and purchase.

Better question than "trial or no trial?":
how much intent does this user already have?

If you run Apple Ads, I would test this before assuming trials are always the move:

  • separate campaigns by country
  • use exact match first
  • match screenshots to the search intent
  • watch revenue by keyword, not only CPI
  • try a no-trial soft paywall if the keyword is high intent
