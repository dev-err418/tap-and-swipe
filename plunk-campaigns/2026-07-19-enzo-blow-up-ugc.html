The UGC system behind 250M views a month

Hey, it's Arthur :)

I just published a new Tap & Swipe episode with Enzo.

A few years ago he was delivering pizzas at Domino's. Today his app business makes around $300K/month.

His main acquisition channel is a small team of UGC creators generating roughly 250 million views every month across TikTok and Instagram.

The surprising part is that Enzo does not ask his creators to be creative.

His team finds formats already proven to work, writes the scripts, and gives creators exact instructions. Then they repeat the format across creators and countries until it stops working.

Some notes I took from the interview:

  • Enzo hires for energy and consistency, not follower count. Some creators started with around 200 followers and reached 100K within two months.
  • One person on the team spends a lot of time finding viral formats inside and outside their niche. The format is adapted to the app before creators receive it.
  • Around 80-90% of creators are paid based on views with no base fee. Bonuses reward cumulative performance, so creators always have a visible next target.
  • The winning platform depends on the market. Instagram drives most of their US revenue, while face-camera TikToks work better in France.
  • A recent onboarding rebuild increased conversion and revenue by 50% without adding any traffic.

"It's not really the right creators. It's the right formats."

I think this is where a lot of app founders get UGC wrong.

They hire a creator, give them the app, and wait for an original idea. Enzo first proves the idea himself. The creator's job is to execute it consistently.

The full episode also gets into how his team sends 400 targeted DMs a day, how creator payouts and bonuses work, why they advertise some features before building them, and how their web-to-app funnel makes around $30K/month.
