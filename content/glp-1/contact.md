# Contact GLP-1

## Need help?

If you have a question about GLP-1, found a bug, or need help with privacy or terms requests, contact Tap & Swipe:

**support@tap-and-swipe.com**

We typically reply within 1 to 2 business days.

## Medical questions

GLP-1 is not a medical service and we cannot answer personal medical questions, interpret symptoms, recommend medication, or tell you whether to change your dose. For health decisions, contact your prescribing clinician, pharmacist, or another qualified healthcare professional.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
